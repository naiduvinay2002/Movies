Captain America: Civil War

In Captain America: Civil War, the Avengers fracture into two opposing factions over political pressure to sign the Sokovia Accords, a system of government oversight. Steve Rogers opposes the control, while Tony Stark supports accountability. A vengeful mastermind, Baron Zemo, manipulates the rift by framing Bucky Barnes, leading to an all-out ideological and physical war between former allies.
